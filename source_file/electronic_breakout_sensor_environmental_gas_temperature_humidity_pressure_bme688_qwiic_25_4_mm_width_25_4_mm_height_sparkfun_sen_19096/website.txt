Overview
Product Overview
The SparkFun BME688 Environmental Sensor is a breakout that combines a gas sensor with temperature, humidity, and barometric pressure sensing in a single package. The BME688's gas sensor can detect a wide variety of volatile organic compounds (VOCs), volatile sulfur compounds (VSCs), and other gases such as carbon monoxide and hydrogen in the part-per-billion (ppb) range. Combine that with precise temperature, humidity, and barometric pressure, and the BME688 can work as a completely standalone environmental sensor all in a 1in. x 1in. breakout!
Solderless Environmental Tracking
The BME688 serves as a drop-in replacement for the BME680 and builds on the previous sensor by adding intelligent gas-scanning capabilities. While standard sensors simply detect the presence of gases, this Bosch sensor generates unique profiles for different gas mixtures. Because we routed the default I
2
C interface to our solderless Qwiic connectors, integrating this powerhouse sensor into your existing setup is incredibly fast and error-free. If you prefer a traditional breadboard layout or need the versatility of SPI communication, all essential pins are conveniently broken out to standard 0.1-inch spaced headers.
Key Performance Capabilities
To ensure your air quality monitoring projects are as precise and efficient as possible, the BME688 Environmental Sensor provides several standout features:
Intelligent Gas Sensing:
Scans for VOCs, VSCs (like hydrogen sulfide), and other gases in the part-per-billion range, featuring an H₂S scanning F1 score of 0.92.
Precision Measurements:
Delivers highly accurate temperature (±1.0°C), relative humidity (±3%), and barometric pressure (±60Pa) data.
Low Power Draw:
Consumes just 0.15 µA in sleep mode and typically only 3.9 mA during a standard 10.8-second gas scan.
