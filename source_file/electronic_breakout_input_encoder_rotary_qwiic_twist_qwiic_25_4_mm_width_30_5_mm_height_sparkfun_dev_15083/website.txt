Product Overview
The SparkFun Qwiic Twist is a digital RGB rotary encoder breakout that adds a simple volume knob or menu selector to your next project without the typical hassle of rotary encoders. This board takes care of all the various interrupts, switches, PWM'ing of LEDs, and presents all those features over an easy-to-use I
2
C interface thanks to our
Qwiic Connect System
! The Qwiic Twist was designed to get rid of the large mass of wires that are needed to implement an RGB encoder on a breadboard. This lets you stop messing around with interrupt debugging and get back to your project!
While at its core the Qwiic Twist is a basic rotary encoder breakout, it also adds a built-in momentary button and an RGB LED for custom lighting indications.
Stop Debugging, Start Building
Adding a physical dial shouldn't mean sacrificing microcontroller pins or fighting interrupt timing. This breakout handles the math for you. As you turn the 6mm shaft, the indent encoder provides an audible and tactile "click." It detects clockwise and counter-clockwise movements that track 24 steps per full rotation and transmits that data clearly over I
2
C.
Need to select a menu item? Just press down on the shaft to activate the built-in button so you can select options on a GUI menu or element or activate pre-programmed executables. Since the Qwiic Twist uses a 6mm shaft, it works great with our
Clear Plastic Knob
or
any other knob
that can fit over a 6mm pole. Keep in mind that if you use an opaque or translucent knob, you may lose some (or all) of the RGB LED's color quality.
Custom Colors and Expansion
Because the SparkFun Qwiic Twist is equipped with dual Qwiic connectors, you can plug this board directly into your system without any soldering. And it includes several features that allow it to fit perfectly into your custom interface:
16 Million Colors:
The integrated red, green, and blue LEDs are PWM-controlled via software on the breakout, which lets you mix custom colors for unique visual feedback.
Scalable Addressing:
The default 0x3F I
2
C address is software configurable. You can daisy-chain up to 111 Qwiic Twists on a single bus to build expansive custom control panels. We also added a solder jumper on the back of the board to physically swap the address to 0x3E.
Flexible Hardware:
While Qwiic connectors make it plug-and-play, we also break out standard 0.1-inch spaced pins for those who prefer a more traditional setup.
The SparkFun Qwiic Twist provides you with an interactive and illuminated dial that simplifies your coding while still keeping your hardware layout clean and professional-looking. With comprehensive Arduino library and Python package support to handle the heavy lifting, the Qwiic Twist is the absolute easiest way to add rotary control to your interface!
